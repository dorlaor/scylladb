7. Findings

Cache Efficiency

The SLRU is working correctly:
  • Protected segment consistently holds 78-81% of cache entries
  • Probation serves as a proper staging area (15-20%)
  • Window stays small (1-4%) as intended for scan-heavy workloads
  • During Zipfian 0.8 + scan, the admission filter rejected ~80% of entries, protecting hot data

Hill Climbing Behavior

Improvements from this session's fixes:
  • Step size = 1% of total: Produces visible hit rate changes (~870 entries/step vs 60 before)
  • Window-only adjustment: Protected stays at 80% default regardless of climber direction
  • 1% floor: Window can't collapse below 1%, ensuring entries have residence time for re-access
  • No oscillation: Unlike the earlier bug where max_window ping-ponged between 1 and 722
Remaining limitations:
  • Slow adaptation: The climb cycle runs every ~44s (sketch reset interval). It takes 5-10 cycles (3-7 minutes) to make meaningful adjustments.
  • No response to Zipfian shift: Switching from s=0.8 to s=0.2 should ideally grow the window (LRU is fine when access is uniform), but the climber can't detect this because hit rate noise drowns the signal.
  • Decay erases progress: The 0.98x decay means step size halves every 35 cycles (~25 minutes). Long-term the climber settles near its current position regardless of workload.
  • One-dimensional search: The climber only adjusts window size. It can't independently tune protected vs probation ratio, or adjust the sketch reset frequency.
