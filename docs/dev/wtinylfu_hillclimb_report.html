Hill Climbing Bug: Oscillation Without Convergence

The hill climber's step size starts at 6.25% of total cache (~4700 entries), which is much larger than the window target (722 for 2%). One step overshoots from "small window" to "tiny window" and the next reversal overshoots to "large window". The decay rate (0.98) is too slow to converge within the 30s sample window.

Caffeine avoids this because its cache has a fixed capacity and the step size is bounded relative to that fixed capacity. In ScyllaDB, total_size fluctuates with LSA pressure, making the step size unstable.
